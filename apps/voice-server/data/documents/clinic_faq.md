# Clinic FAQ & Knowledge Base

## Insurance & Billing
- We accept BlueCross, Aetna, Medicare, and UnitedHealthcare.
- We do not accept Medicaid at this time.
- Co-pays are due at the time of your visit. We accept credit cards and Apple Pay.

## Location & Hours
- We are located at 123 Health Way, Austin, Texas 78701.
- Our hours are 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM, Monday through Friday.
- We are closed on weekends and federal holidays.

## Appointments & Services
- We offer both in-person visits and virtual telehealth appointments.
- Walk-ins are accepted for urgent care, but appointments are highly recommended to avoid long wait times.
- If you need to cancel, please do so at least 24 hours in advance to avoid a $50 cancellation fee.
